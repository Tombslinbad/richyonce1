## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal sophistication, structural power, and artisanal mastery of contemporary African haute couture. Drawing aesthetic cues from high-fashion editorial publications, classical African tailoring, and avant-garde architectural silhouettes, the interface establishes an atmosphere of exclusive bespoke luxury.

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Glassmorphism and Hairline Precision**:
- Monolithic structures set against deep, jewel-toned dark canvases.
- Micro-fine metallic hairlines that reflect bespoke garment seams and precision pattern cutting.
- Expansive, cinematic typography pairing dramatic transitional serifs with austere, spaced geometric sans-serifs.
- Tactile, translucent panels offering layered depth akin to silk organza and woven damask.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an opulent nocturnal spectrum, evoking bespoke ateliers illuminated by warm golden accents.

- **Primary (`#E5C07B` - Champagne Gold):** Used with restraint for focal CTAs, refined micro-borders, active status indicators, and iconic monogram details. It conveys prestige and rare craftsmanship.
- **Secondary (`#0A231C` - Forest Emerald):** The foundational depth color, invoking rich vegetable-dyed velvets and African heritage forestry. Applied to hero sections, atmospheric backdrops, and interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#C5B499` - Warm Muted Sand):** The secondary voice for editorial supporting details, secondary copy, category tags, and muted rules.
- **Neutral (`#0D0D0D` - Deep Obsidian):** The primary canvas ground, creating cinematic contrast against metallic and organic pigments.
- **Surface Accent (`#112520` - Obsidian Emerald):** Elevated card tiers and floating modals, bridging pure obsidian with verdant undertones.
- **Contrast Text (`#FAFAFA` - Pure Chalk):** Editorial body copy, stark headlines, and critical status elements requiring crisp readability against dark surfaces.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual architecture:

- **Playfair Display** delivers haute-couture editorial presence. Used exclusively for display moments, collection titles, lookbook markers, and editorial pull quotes. It retains natural letter spacing and balanced proportional metrics to maintain romantic classicism.
- **Inter** provides structural, utilitarian equilibrium. All metadata, luxury badges, product specifications, navigation anchors, and interactive labels must be formatted in **all-caps with wide tracking (`0.15em` to `0.22em`)**.
- Body copy remains airy and lightweight (300-400 weight), allowing photography and garments to dominate the view.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model relies on a 12-column editorial asymmetric grid on desktop, transitioning to 6 columns on tablet and 2 to 4 columns on mobile.

- **Editorial Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` and extended multi-token section pads) allow individual pieces to be perceived as curated artifacts rather than standard commodity listings.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Lookbook layouts and product spotlights frequently adopt offset column spans (e.g., a 7-column hero garment paired with a 4-column atelier narrative offset by 1 blank column).
- **Adaptive Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** Minimalist 1.25rem outer margins to maximize visual real estate for vertical garment imagery. Single or staggered two-column grids.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 3rem margins, standard gutters, 6-column fluid structure.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 5rem margins, asymmetric 12-column system with bespoke whitespace tension.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering, ambient glow, and translucent veil surfaces rather than standard drop shadows:

- **Tonal Layering:** The foundational layer sits at `#0D0D0D`. Elevated surfaces shift to `#112520` (Obsidian Emerald) or `#0A231C` with subtle linear gradients to `#0D0D0D`.
- **Atelier Glassmorphism:** Overlays, navigation headers, lookbook panels, and quick-view drawers employ `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` combined with a background of `rgba(13, 13, 13, 0.72)`.
- **Gold Hairline Borders:** Structural containers and interactive frames utilize an ultra-fine 1px border colored `rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.22)`. On hover or focus, this activates to `rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.65)`.
- **Ambient Illumination:** High-tier surfaces project an atmospheric ambient glow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(10, 35, 28, 0.65), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language.

- **Architectural Precision:** Every interactive element, image frame, surface container, input field, and modal uses razor-sharp corners (0px border-radius).
- This crisp geometry mirrors the angular drapery, architectural shoulder cuts, and crisp lapels characteristic of luxury bespoke tailoring.
- Circular geometry is strictly reserved for minute functional cues (e.g., color swatch circles and radio select dots).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Atelier Button:** Solid `#E5C07B` fill with `#0D0D0D` uppercase label (`label-md`). Zero corner radius. On hover, background transitions to `#FAFAFA` with subtle champagne luminescence.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px border of `rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.35)`, `#FAFAFA` text. On hover, border illuminates to solid `#E5C07B`, text shifts to `#E5C07B`.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Pure Chalk uppercase typography (`label-lg`) anchored by an animated 1px hairline underline that expands from center-out on hover.

### Cards & Garment Showcases
- **Product Tiles:** Sharp rectangular frame with a background of `#112520`. Integrated 1px hairline perimeter (`rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.12)`). Images feature a subtle zoom transition on interaction.
- **Atelier Storytelling Cards:** Two-tone split cards pairing editorial photography alongside rich Obsidian Emerald panels detailing textile origin, fabric weight, and tailoring notes.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs:** Borderless top, left, and right; single 1px solid bottom hairline of `rgba(197, 180, 153, 0.35)`. Transparent background. Label floats above using `label-sm` in Warm Muted Sand. On focus, bottom hairline illuminates to `#E5C07B`.
- **Measurements & Bespoke Selectors:** Segmented grid selectors enclosed in razor-edged 1px boxes with monospaced sizing and uppercase tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio Buttons:** Sharp 14px outer square with a 1px border of `rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.4)`. Active state features a centered 6px solid Champagne Gold square.
- **Checkboxes:** 14px crisp square with hairline outline. Active state displays an angular, gold tick.

### Chips & Badges
- **Collection Badges:** Pill-less, sharp rectangular chips with 1px border of `rgba(229, 192, 123, 0.3)`. Background uses `rgba(17, 37, 32, 0.6)`. Typography strictly set in `label-sm` with Warm Sand text.

### Navigation & Lookbook Drawers
- **Header:** Sticky, frosted glass bar (`rgba(13, 13, 13, 0.8)` with blur) with a 1px hairline bottom divider in gold.
- **Bespoke Measurement Drawer:** Slide-in panel in `#112520` with sharp borders, designed for configuring garment customizations and booking private atelier appointments.
